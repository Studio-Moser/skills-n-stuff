# House Rules

Harness owns the canonical rules at
[`plugins/harness/references/house-rules.md`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Studio-Moser/skills-n-stuff/main/plugins/harness/references/house-rules.md).
Read that reference in full and follow it; do not reconstruct or summarize a second
copy here. A repository's own `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md` still wins where it
explicitly overrides the universal rules.
